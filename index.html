Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join this course?
Anyone can join — any age, any gender, and any educational qualification. No prior technical background is required.
Is this course suitable after 10th?
Yes. Students who have completed 10th standard can join and learn mobile repairing as a practical, income-generating skill.
Is this course suitable after 12th?
Yes. It is a great option after 12th if you want a hands-on skill alongside or instead of further studies.
Can girls join this course?
Absolutely. MRI welcomes students of any gender, and many of our successful graduates are women.
Is prior technical knowledge required?
No. The course is designed to start from the basics, so complete beginners can follow along comfortably.
Will I get a certificate?
Yes, you receive a Government Certified Certificate as well as an MRI Institute Certificate on completion.
Are tools included?
Yes, mobile repairing tools are included as part of the course benefits.
Is there practical training on real devices?
Yes, 80% of the course is hands-on practice on real, working iPhone and Android devices.
What is the course duration?
The Regular Track runs for 90 days, and the Fast Track is a 45-day intensive program.
Is lifetime support available?
Yes, MRI offers lifetime support so you can reach out with questions even after finishing the course.
Can I start my own business after this course?
Yes, many students go on to start their own repair shops or service centers using the skills and market guidance learned at MRI.
How can I book the free demo class?
Fill out the booking form on this website, or message us on WhatsApp or call us directly to schedule your free demo.
What is the difference between Basic, Regular and Fast Track?
Basic (₹9,999) covers basic repairing only over 90 days. Regular Track (₹29,999) is the complete hardware, glass and marketing curriculum over 90 days with tools, certificate and job assistance included. Fast Track (₹39,999) covers the same complete curriculum as Regular Track in an intensive 45-day schedule.
Is job assistance really provided?
Yes. Regular Track and Fast Track students get referrals to repair shops and service centers in our network, interview guidance, and support if you choose to start your own shop instead.
What happens after the limited-time offer ends?
The Regular Track is priced at ₹50,000. The current ₹29,999 offer is available for a limited time — book your seat through the form or WhatsApp to lock in today's price.
